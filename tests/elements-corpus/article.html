Smart boxes

Hovering a window offers the panel under the pointer. Scrolling widens the box to the panel around it, and on to the whole window. Dragging still selects any rectangle you like.
VisualLooks for borders and background changes in the frozen image.
RegionsAlso finds cards, fields and tabs inside panels.
AccessibilityAsks the application what is under the pointer.
gsettings set org.gnome.shell.extensions.capit element-detection regions
make reload
